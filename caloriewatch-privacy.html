CalorieWatch Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026  |  Developer: Enrique Assad  |  View User Agreement →

CalorieWatch ("the App", package name com.kikeassad.caloriewatch) is a personal metabolic, calorie deficit, and fitness tracking Android application designed to work with Huawei Watch 3 and Huawei Health Service Kit (HMS Health Kit). Protecting your personal and health data privacy is our highest priority.

1. Health & Fitness Data Accessed via Huawei Health Kit

Only after you grant explicit authorization via Huawei ID Sign-In and Huawei Health permission dialogs, CalorieWatch requests read-only access to the following Huawei Health Kit data scopes:

Huawei Health Kit Scope Data Type Read Specific Purpose in CalorieWatch
Read Step Count
DT_CONTINUOUS_STEPS_DELTA		 Daily step counts and time intervals Calculates non-workout daily activity burn (NEAT) and displays daily/weekly/monthly/yearly step statistics.
Read Calories Burnt
DT_CONTINUOUS_CALORIES_BURNT		 Active & continuous calories burned Computes Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) and compares Calories Out against Calories In.
Read Activity Records
ActivityRecord		 Workout type, start/end timestamps, active calories, steps Displays Huawei Watch 3 workout sessions and subtracts workout windows from background steps to prevent double-counting.
Read Heart Rate
DT_INSTANTANEOUS_HEART_RATE		 Workout average heart rate (bpm) Displays exercise intensity alongside synced Huawei Watch 3 workout summaries.
Read Body Weight
DT_INSTANTANEOUS_BODY_WEIGHT		 Body weight records (kg) Updates Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) calculations and plots raw vs. 7-day EMA weight trends.

2. Local-Only Storage & Zero Third-Party Sharing

  • 100% On-Device Storage: All health metrics synced from Huawei Health Kit and all manually entered calorie/weight logs are stored exclusively in a local SQLite/Room database on your Android phone.
  • No External Cloud Transmission: CalorieWatch does not operate external backend servers and never uploads your health data to third-party servers.
  • No Selling or Advertising Use: Your health and fitness data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
  • Non-Medical Purpose: CalorieWatch is a general wellness and fitness tracking tool and does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical treatment.

3. How to Revoke Authorization & Delete Your Data

  • Revoking Huawei Health Kit Access: You may revoke CalorieWatch's access to Huawei Health Kit at any time by opening the Huawei Health app → Me → Privacy Management → Data Source Management / Connected Apps → selecting CalorieWatch and revoking permissions.
  • Deleting Local Data: You can delete individual calorie or workout entries directly inside the CalorieWatch app, or clear all local data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → CalorieWatch → Storage → Clear Data (or by uninstalling the application).
